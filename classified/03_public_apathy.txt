The thing that finally clicked for me: everyone's looking for
Kapatapuram in the wrong place because they're anchoring on the city,
not the water. If Pahruli really carried what the old accounts
describe, the debris field isn't at the city's supposed site at all.
It's downstream, wherever the valley emptied out.

I keep coming back to how the same sources describe Pahruli before any
of this — clear, cold, loud in the monsoon and nearly silent the rest
of the year. People wrote about swimming in it. About it being the
first sound you'd hear walking into the valley. Whatever happened to
Kapatapuram, it took that away too, and almost nobody who tells this
story remembers to mention that part.
