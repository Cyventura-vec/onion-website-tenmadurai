The place I think the street-academy is describing — if I'm right — is
past the current shoreline southwest of Kanyakumari, along the old
shelf break. I've looked at enough depth charts now that this isn't a
guess anymore, it's a shortlist.

I keep thinking about what it must have looked like before. The
Pahruli was supposed to run right past there, wide and slow, the kind
of river people built their whole lives around instead of just near.
Every account I've found describes it the same way — not as a
resource, but as something people loved. That's rare, in accounts this
old. Most of what survives is about kings and cities. This is the only
place anyone bothered writing about the water itself.
