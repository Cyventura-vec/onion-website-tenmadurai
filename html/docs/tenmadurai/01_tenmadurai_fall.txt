I've spent the better part of two years on this, mostly alone, and I
think I've been wrong to keep it that way.

The Tenmadurai accounts aren't just old stories to me anymore. The
detail about scholars going unheard, about a city that had no one left
to teach the next generation what it knew — I can't stop seeing that
pattern everywhere I look, in every account of what came after it too.

I'm not a historian. I'm not an archaeologist. I'm someone who noticed
something and couldn't put it down. If you know how to read old survey
data, old coastal maps, anything — I could use the help. I'll share
what I have.

— K.D.
