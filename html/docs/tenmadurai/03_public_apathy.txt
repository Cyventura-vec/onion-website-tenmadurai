The part I keep returning to isn't the city's decline. It's the part
where the scholars just kept going anyway — smaller crowds, some days
no one at all, and still they opened the manuscripts.

I don't think they were naive about it. I think they knew exactly what
was happening. They kept going anyway because they'd decided it was
worth doing regardless of whether anyone was watching.

I find that useful, honestly. There are a lot of days doing this
alone when I need to remember that part.
