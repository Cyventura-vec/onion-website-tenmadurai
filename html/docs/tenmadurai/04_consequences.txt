What finally disappears isn't the knowledge itself. It's the last
person who could explain what the words actually meant — not translate
them literally, but explain what was being pointed at, what the person
who wrote them was actually trying to say.

Once that person is gone, the words are still there. They just stop
meaning anything to anyone left to read them.
